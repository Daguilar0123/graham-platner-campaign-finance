Outside Money in the 2026 Maine Senate Race: Graham Platner

Between October 2025 and Graham Platner's exit from the race on July 8, 2026, outside groups reported $11.4 million in independent expenditures about his candidacy to the Federal Election Commission — and the split was lopsided: roughly $34 was spent opposing him for every $1 spent supporting him. Nearly all of the opposing money came through a single super PAC, Pine Tree Results PAC, paying a single media vendor, and part of that PAC's funding traces to 501(c)(4) nonprofits that do not disclose their donors. This page lists every FEC-filed independent expenditure for or against Platner, each linked to the actual filing, along with the reported money flows that never appear in FEC independent-expenditure data.
