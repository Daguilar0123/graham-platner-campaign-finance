What Can $11 Million Buy?

It just might have bought the derailment of a people-powered movement.

Between October 2025 and Graham Platner's exit from the race on July 8, 2026, outside groups reported $11.4 million in independent expenditures about his candidacy to the Federal Election Commission. Nearly all of the opposing money came through a single super PAC, Pine Tree Results PAC, paying a single media vendor — and part of that PAC's funding traces to 501(c)(4) nonprofits that do not disclose their donors. This page lists every FEC-filed independent expenditure for or against Platner, each linked to the actual filing, along with the reported money flows that never appear in FEC independent-expenditure data.

Where the opposing money went

One super PAC — Pine Tree Results PAC — accounts for $10,723,548 of the $11,077,173 in opposition spending: six media buys, all paid to a single vendor, SRCP Media. Three smaller committees make up the remainder.
